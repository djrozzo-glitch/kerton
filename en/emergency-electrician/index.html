When to call

Call us right away if

  • Power keeps trippingA circuit or the RCD trips again and again.
  • Short circuitA bang, spark or flash at a socket, switch or appliance.
  • Burning smellYou smell burning near the meter cupboard, a socket or a light.
  • Warm or discoloured socketsScorch marks or a socket that feels warm.
  • Water near the electricsAfter a leak near sockets, lights or the meter cupboard.
  • No power, only at your placeThe neighbours have power and your circuits won't switch back on.

Costs

What determines the cost?

For a fault, the cost mainly depends on:

  • the time: daytime, evening, night or weekend
  • the cause and how long tracing it takes
  • any parts that need replacing
  • the distance to your address

Feel free to ask about the cost on the phone before we come out.

FAQ

Questions about faults

When do I call the grid operator and when an electrician?

Are the neighbours out too? Then it is a grid outage: call 0800-9009. Only your home without power, or a circuit that keeps tripping? Then the problem is in your own installation and you call us.

How fast can you be there?

As soon as possible. Exactly how fast depends on where you live and how busy it is. You get an estimate on the phone.

My RCD keeps tripping. What can I do myself?

Switch off all circuits under that RCD, switch it back on, then switch the circuits on one by one. Trips on one circuit? Unplug everything there and try again. Still tripping? Call us.

Do you come out at night and at weekends?

Yes. The fault service is available 24/7, weekends included.
