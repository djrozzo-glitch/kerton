FAQ

Questions about electrics and emergencies

Which area do you cover?

We work within roughly 100 km of The Hague: mainly The Hague, Rotterdam, Delft, Leiden, Zoetermeer, Gouda, Amsterdam, Haarlem, Utrecht and the surrounding towns.

Are you really available 24/7 for emergencies?

Yes. For a power outage, short circuit or dangerous situation you can call 085 333 48 44 day and night, weekends included.

What should I do during a short circuit or power outage?

Check the meter cupboard to see whether the RCD or a circuit has tripped. If it trips again straight away, you smell burning or see damage, leave that circuit off, don't touch anything and call us.

Can you replace my fuse box or meter cupboard?

Yes. We replace old fuse boxes with a new distribution board and upgrade the electrics in the meter cupboard, for example for an induction hob, a Perilex connection or an EV charger.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes. We install the charger and take care of the connection in the meter cupboard.

How do I get a quote or book a visit?

Call 085 333 48 44 or send a WhatsApp to +31 6 86467613. A photo of the meter cupboard or the problem helps us quickly see what's needed.
