Fuse box replacement

An old fuse box is often why circuits keep tripping, or why there is no room for an induction hob, EV charger or heat pump. We replace or extend your fuse box across The Hague and the Randstad.

Signs

Is your fuse box due for replacement?

  • Older than about 25 yearsOlder boards were not built for today's power use.
  • Screw-in fusesStill fuses instead of circuit breakers? Replacing is the sensible choice.
  • No RCD or only oneA modern board spreads circuits over several RCDs.
  • Circuits trip oftenEspecially when several heavy appliances run at once.
  • Power strips everywhereToo few circuits usually means too many appliances on one.
  • New heavy appliancesInduction hobs, EV chargers, heat pumps or solar panels need their own circuits.

FAQ

Fuse box questions

How long does a fuse box replacement take?

For an average home it usually takes part of a day. The exact time depends on the number of circuits and the condition of the wiring.

Will I be without power during the work?

Yes, temporarily. The power has to be off during the replacement. We tell you upfront roughly how long.

Can I add circuits instead of replacing everything?

Sometimes. If there is room in the board and it is in good condition, extending it can be enough. A photo often tells us already.

Do I need a three-phase connection?

An induction hob, EV charger or heat pump sometimes needs a heavier connection. You request that from your grid operator. We tell you whether it is needed in your situation.
