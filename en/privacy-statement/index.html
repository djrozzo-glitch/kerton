Privacy statement

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

Kerton Onderhoudsbedrijf (Chamber of Commerce/KvK 90056310), based in The Hague, is the controller for the processing of personal data described in this privacy statement.

Questions about privacy? Call 085 333 48 44 or send a WhatsApp to +31 6 86467613.

What data we process

When you call us, send a WhatsApp or give us a job, we process the data you give us yourself:

  • name and phone number;
  • the address where the work takes place;
  • a description of the problem and any photos you send, for example of your meter cupboard;
  • email address and invoicing details, if you provide them.

On this website we also process usage data, see Cookies and analytics.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Quotes and carrying out the job: contacting you, scheduling and doing the work. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Accounting and invoicing: legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Website analytics: measuring how the site is used and how many people call or message via the site. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 only if you consent in the cookie notice. Only then are the cookies _ga and _ga_* set (kept for up to 2 years). Among other things we measure page views and clicks on the call and WhatsApp buttons.

If you do not consent, we set no analytics or advertising cookies. Google may still receive limited cookieless signals (Google Consent Mode) that do not identify you as a visitor.

Your choice is stored in your own browser (kerton-consent in local storage). You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Other services on this website

  • Hosting: the site runs on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). On every visit GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, including for security.
  • Fonts: we load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address reaches Google.
  • WhatsApp: messages you send via WhatsApp go through WhatsApp (Meta), whose own privacy policy also applies.

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We only share it with the parties we need for the services above, when needed to carry out your job, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep data

  • Data for quotes and jobs: no longer than needed for the job and any warranty or follow-up questions.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.
  • Analytics data in Google Analytics: up to 14 months.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict processing, to object and to data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights; we respond within one month.

Not happy with how we handle your data? You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Security

The website uses a secure connection (HTTPS). We take appropriate measures to protect your data against loss and unauthorised use.

Changes

We may update this privacy statement. The latest version is always on this page.
